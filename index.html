A Heritage of Cattle.

For 40 years, Whitley Farms has been raising quality cattle and building feed programs that help our customers succeed. We run a mama cow/calf operation, pre-condition stocker calves for the feed yards, develop custom feed mixtures, and broker feed by the truckload.

Family values, practical experience, and honest business guide everything we do. When you work with Whitley Farms, you're working with people who are out there doing it themselves — every single day.
